Affiliate disclosure — the part you probably came for

FreeHush participates in the affiliate programs of the paid tools it covers: Krisp (via the Impact network), Adobe (via Partnerize), and Auphonic (via FastSpring). Your price doesn't change. Every page containing such links carries a disclosure note near the top, and this page is the canonical roster: if a vendor isn't named here, we have no relationship with it.

What commissions don't buy: the completely free tools in our rankings (NVIDIA Broadcast, Audacity, OBS) pay us nothing and outrank paid options wherever they're genuinely better; our standard advice is to stay free; and every review states the free tier's limits before mentioning any paid plan.
